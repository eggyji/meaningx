Who we are

What is MeaningX

MeaningX is a research and practice collective working at the intersection of design and AI. We hold that the meaning of what technology produces is not computed, but designed. Accordingly, we do two things: build a design-driven, cross-disciplinary cluster of agents, and cultivate an open benchmark community that measures the plural values of design.

OpenStudio Manifesto

Evaluating for plurality
I

No Single Right Answer

“What counts as good design” is itself a design question. We refuse to compress design into a single score or a loss curve. The task of a benchmark is to expose disagreement, not to erase it — the leaderboard should have ties at first place, together with their reasons for objecting to one another.

II

Antidisciplinary by Default

Evaluating design agents requires designers, engineers, anthropologists, and real users at the same table. Disciplinary boundaries are meant to be crossed, not hidden behind. The jury is always larger than the lab.

III

Values Before Metrics

Every benchmark quantifies something someone cares about. Declare what you care about before claiming to measure it well. Quantification exists to make values discussable — not to make value judgments on our behalf.

IV

Process as Evidence

We likewise evaluate how results are reached: the breadth of exploration, the reasoning behind trade-offs, the options set aside. A transparent process merits the same score as a beautiful outcome.

V

Open & Reproducible

Tasks, data, and grading rationales are all open. We can be outperformed; we refuse to be a black box. Any agent’s score must be fully replayable — and challengeable — within the community.
